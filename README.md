# Netflix-Monetization-Intelligence
Netflix Ad Monetization Analytics Dashboard
Overview
The Netflix Ad Monetization Analytics Dashboard is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project designed to analyze how viewer behavior, content preferences, audience demographics, and subscription plans impact advertising performance and revenue generation on Netflix's ad-supported platform.

As streaming services increasingly adopt ad-supported subscription models, understanding the relationship between content consumption and advertising effectiveness has become critical for maximizing revenue while maintaining a positive viewer experience. This dashboard provides data-driven insights that help identify high-performing content categories, valuable audience segments, and profitable subscription tiers.

📋 Short Description:-

A Power BI and MySQL project analyzing Netflix's ad-supported business model by exploring genre revenue performance, audience engagement, binge-watching behavior, and subscription profitability to uncover key ad monetization insights.

Business Problem :- 
Netflix generates advertising revenue by delivering targeted advertisements to users on ad-supported plans. To optimize monetization strategies, the platform needs to answer several key business questions:

📊 Which content genre generates the highest advertising revenue?

🍿 Does binge-watching increase ad engagement?

👥 Which age group clicks ads the most?

💳 Which subscription tier is the most profitable?


Project Objectives:-

Analyze Netflix viewer behavior and content consumption patterns.

Measure advertising effectiveness using Click-Through Rate (CTR) and engagement metrics.

Identify audience segments with the highest advertising value.

Evaluate genre-level revenue performance.

Assess subscription plan profitability.

Provide actionable insights to improve ad monetization strategies.



✨Dashboard  Features & Highlights

📈 Executive Overview :
     -Consolidated KPIs and business insights for decision-makers.
     
🎭 Genre Revenue Analytics :
     -Revenue, CTR, watch time, and viewer performance by content genre.

🍿 Binge-Watching Analysis :
     -Comparison of binge-watchers and regular viewers based on engagement and advertising performance.

👤 Audience Monetization Analysis :
     -Age-group-wise ad interactions, clicks, and CTR performance.

💰 Subscription Profitability Analysis :
     -Revenue and engagement comparison across subscription tiers.


🛠️ Tech Stack:-

MySQL :– Data cleaning, transformation, and data modeling

Power BI :– Interactive dashboard development

DAX :– KPI calculations and business measures

SQL :– Data analysis and business reporting



📈Business Impact:-

This dashboard demonstrates how Netflix can leverage viewer behavior and advertising analytics to optimize content strategy, 
improve audience targeting, and maximize ad revenue. By combining content performance, audience engagement, and subscription 
insights, the project showcases a data-driven approach to advertising monetization in the OTT streaming industry.
